How it works

From a workbook on your desktop to an app on every phone in the building, in a few minutes.

Drop in your workbook

Sheetsmith reads every sheet: tables, formulas, dropdowns and checks, conditional colours, buttons and the VBA behind them.

Shape the app

Rename screens, pick columns and types, choose which macros become buttons. Preview the real app as a phone or a laptop.

Run it on your network

Download a zip, double-click the start file, and open the address it shows on every device on your Wi-Fi.

What comes across

The parts of a workbook that make it an app, not just a table.

Tables become apps

Search, filter chips, sorting, cards on phones and a form for every row, with totals rows and calculated columns.

Formulas keep working

calculate live: XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, INDEX/MATCH, dates, text and table references.

Rules stay rules

Dropdown lists, number and date limits and custom checks, with the error messages you wrote in Excel.

Same colours

Conditional formats colour rows, cards and chips just like the sheet: late orders red, ready ones green.

Macros that really run

Buttons run your VBA in the browser: Range, Cells, ListObjects, loops, MsgBox, InputBox, Dictionary, events.

Forms and calculators

Sheets with labels and input cells become forms, with the results updating as people type.

Shared and live

Everyone on the network sees changes within seconds. The activity list shows who changed what.

Back to Excel any time

One click exports the current data as an .xlsx with its tables and formulas.

Questions

Does my team need to install anything?

No. Only the computer that shares the app needs Python (free, and already on Macs and Linux). Everyone else just opens an address in their browser.

Is my workbook stored?

When you try Sheetsmith without an account, the file is read and set aside: nothing is kept. When you save a project, the workbook is stored in your account so you can change the app later. You can delete it any time.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Once downloaded it runs entirely on your network: the server, the formulas and the macros all run on your own computers.

Which VBA works?

The everyday kind: subs and functions, If/Select/For/Do, Range, Cells, worksheets, tables (ListObjects), MsgBox and InputBox, Dictionary, string and date functions, WorksheetFunction, and the Worksheet_Change event. The Macros tab checks every procedure and tells you exactly what would not run.

Can several people change things at once?

Yes. Each change is sent to the server and everyone else sees it within a few seconds. Rows are kept separately, so two people adding orders at the same time don't overwrite each other.

How big can the workbook be?

Up to 12 MB, with up to 3,000 rows per sheet. That covers most workbooks people use as apps; bigger data is better off in a real database.

Is it secure?

The app is meant for networks you trust (home, office, shop). Anyone on the network can open it unless you set a PIN. It never sends your data anywhere.
